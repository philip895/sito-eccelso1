About us

A housemade of light.

Eccelso is a small collection of rooms and suites in Palermo, for people who want the city at the door and calm inside.

We think a stay should feel like being at home, only lighter, quieter and a little more beautiful.

01Hospitality

Mornings,without hurry.

White porcelain, a flower on the table, the whole day still ahead. The house is made for slow mornings and unhurried evenings, and for coming back to after a day in the city.

02What matters to us

Three quietprinciples.

I

Light

Rooms that face the light, finished in soft whites so the Sicilian sun can do the rest.

II

Detail

Brass, linen, a softly lit niche, a plant in the right place. Nothing loud, nothing left to chance.

III

Ease

Kitchenettes, room to unpack, a reception to help. Everything you need for a short stay or a long one.

03Around us

City and sea,close by.

Palermo's churches, palaces and markets are all around, and the long beach at Mondello is a short drive away.
