About us

ElegantApartments.

Living in luxury. Bright spaces, quality materials and a welcoming atmosphere make Eccelso the perfect setting for your stay.

Every space has been designed to make you feel at home, with the elegance and comforts of a luxury hotel in Palermo’s historic centre.

01Hospitality

The name Eccelso captures the essence of the property: a refined play on words that recalls Via del Celso, where the residence stands, and the idea of excellence that defines its service, design and finishes.

02What matters to us

Three quietprinciples.

I

Light

Apartments that face the light, finished in soft whites so the Sicilian sun can do the rest.

II

Detail

Fine materials, refined atmospheres and carefully chosen details. A perfect balance of design, harmony and understated luxury.

III

Ease

Kitchenettes, room to unpack, a reception to help. Everything you need for a short stay or a long one.

03Around us

City and sea,close by.

Churches, palaces and markets are all around you, and the La Cala waterfront is just a few minutes on foot.
