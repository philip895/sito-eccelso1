Location

In the heartof Il Capo.

Via del Celso, three minutes on foot from the Cathedral: old Palermo begins just outside the door.

01Palermo

Palermo,at your door.

Via del Celso runs through Il Capo, the old market quarter beside the Cathedral. Arab-Norman domes, baroque churches and street food are all on foot from the door; the sea at Mondello is a short drive away.

02On foot

The old city,around the corner.

  1. 01Palermo CathedralThe Arab-Norman cathedral, around the corner.about 3 min on foot
  2. 02Fontana PretoriaThe great Renaissance fountain of the Piazza Pretoria.about 500 m
  3. 03Teatro MassimoItaly's largest opera house.about 7 min on foot
  4. 04Mercato del CapoThe historic street market that gives the quarter its name.about 5 min on foot
  5. 05Quattro CantiThe baroque crossroads at the heart of the old city.about 6 min on foot
  6. 06Palazzo dei NormanniThe Norman palace and its golden Cappella Palatina.about 10 min on foot
