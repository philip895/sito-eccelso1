Wellness

Slow down,you’re home.

A hot tub, a sauna and a Turkish steam room, a few steps from your room in the heart of Palermo.

01The ritual

Warmth, steamand stillness.

A hot tub raised on sage-green steps, a wooden sauna, a Turkish steam room lit in deep blue, and massage treatments on request. Come back from the city and let the day slow down.

02The spaces

Three waysto unwind.

01 / 03

The hot tub

Warm water, on its own steps.

Raised on sage-green steps beside a wall painted with a botanical garden, with soft robes and a chilled glass within reach.

02 / 03

The sauna

Dry heat, warm wood.

A wooden sauna with a stone heater, bucket and ladle: the classic way to let the heat do its work.

03 / 03

The Turkish steam room

Steam, in deep blue light.

Dark slabs veined with white, a long bench and soft blue light: a quiet room for steam and slow breathing.

Massages,on request.

Massages and beauty treatments can be arranged during your stay. Ask us when you book, or write to us before you arrive.
