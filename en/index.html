Eccelso · Italian for lofty, sublime; from the Latin excelsus.

01The house

Behind an old façade,a new kind of calm.

Step in from the street and the noise of the city falls away. Inside, everything is light: white plaster, brushed brass, botanical murals and the deep green of a living wall. Eccelso is a small house, made for slow days.

Our story

02Rituals

Small luxuries,taken seriously.

Linen folded just so. Soft robes, something cold in a glass. And a few steps from your room, a hot tub, a sauna and a Turkish steam room, for when you want to leave the city outside.
